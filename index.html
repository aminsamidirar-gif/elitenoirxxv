The Academy

A House, not a club.

Elite Noir XXV is the senior chapter of the Elite Kickers lineage — built in Addis Ababa for players who already carry the gold inside them.

There are no scouts at our gates. No open trials. No mass enrolment. Fifty seats across two grades. One cycle. Twelve months under a single roof of obsidian and brass — where the ball is taught as language, and silence as discipline.

We are based at the Imperial Sporting Quarter beside the National Palace grounds. Our pitch is private. Our hours are early. Our methods belong to no federation but the one written on our crest. Players arrive as recommendations and leave as references.

Elite Noir XXV operates as the invitation-only elite counterpart to Elite Kickers Academy — our sister programme, where every Noir candidate begins.

The Programme

Six chapters. One cycle.

A twelve-month curriculum drafted around the player — not the league. Every chapter taught in residence, by a fellow of the house.

IMentorship

One to One

Every player is paired with a senior fellow for the duration of the cycle. Weekly private sessions, daily film, and a personal ledger that follows them after graduation.

IINutrition

The Table

Three meals composed by a resident kitchen. Highland grains, fresh injera, lean game. No supplements, no shortcuts — only what the body asks for that day.

IIIMental

The Quiet Room

Two hours each evening for stillness, reading, and review. Performance psychology, breath, and the art of staying small inside a loud moment.

IVPathway

The Compass

Direct counsel with European, Gulf, and African club networks. We do not promise contracts — we teach the player to be the kind of name a contract finds.

VPhysical

The Forge

Strength, mobility, recovery. Highland-altitude conditioning paired with daily ice, sauna, and physiotherapy. Bodies built to last, not just to peak.

VIAcademic

The Library

Mornings are footballers, afternoons are students. Accredited tutoring in English, mathematics, finance, and the history of the game. We graduate a person.
